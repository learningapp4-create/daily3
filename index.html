  • Dune GlideSteer a marble over a sand dune
  • Ring HopHop rings and catch stars
  • Beetle PathTurn stones so a beetle reaches a flower
  • Pond SkipSkip a stone to the far lily pad
  • Balloon DriftMatch the balloon to the gardens
  • Plank BrookFit planks across the brook
  • Lantern PostSet the lantern on the posts
  • Nest RowMatch each egg to its nest
  • Path GateOpen the bars for the wagon
  • Dock RaftLoad a raft, then cross to the jetty
  • Sun BeamSlide a lamp onto the pale flower
  • Door PipsTurn a key until the door opens
