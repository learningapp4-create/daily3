# Sun Beam

A lamp slides along a rail. Its pool of light finds the pale flower, and that flower stays bright.

## Change the game

Edit `config.js`.

- `spots` — how many pads are on the rail
- `blooms` — how many flowers a round asks for
- `startSpot` — where the lamp begins
- `span` — the distance between pads
- `slideSeconds` — how long the lamp takes to move one pad
- `poolRadius` — how wide the light on the grass is
- `colors` — sky, grass, flower, lit flower, lamp, and light
- `sounds` — files for a step, a bloom, and the end. `soundVolume` turns them down

The clips in `sfx/` were rendered with [earshot](https://github.com/learningapp4-create/dailyearshot).
